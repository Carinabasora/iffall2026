Project Title: The Luna Moth

I’d like to build this site as multiple pages, with simple navigation across the top. The Wikipedia article has clear topic sections and plenty of photography, so each topic can have its own page. The site will be imagery-forward, with a calm tone, muted colors taken from the moth, and short captions. Botanical drawings of the trees the caterpillars feed on can add an organic touch.

  • Home Page/Introduction – Introduce the luna moth and the main idea of the site: it is one of North America’s most striking insects, yet few people ever see one. Set a quiet, gentle tone with a large hero image.
  • Appearance – Pale green wings, eyespots, long tails, and size. Includes differences between males and females, shown in a gallery of close-up photos.
  • Life Cycle – The main page of the site. Egg, caterpillar, cocoon, and adult, presented as a visual step-by-step sequence with short captions.
  • Habitat and Diet – Where the moth lives in eastern North America and which trees the caterpillars eat, such as walnut, hickory, and sweetgum. Botanical drawings of the host trees can be used here.
  • Defenses and Threats – How the moth avoids predators (caterpillar clicking, tails that confuse bats) and the introduced fly that harms its population.
  • Sources – this page will just list sources, including the Wikipedia page, as well as other sources for photography, and any other credits that need to be provided.

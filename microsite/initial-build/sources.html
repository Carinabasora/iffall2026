Color palette

The palette was sampled from reference artwork of the game and kept to a few flat colors, like the game’s own illustrations.

Sky blue The open skies in the game’s landscape scenes.

Mountain green The distant hills and garden backdrops.

Frog lime The frog itself, used for the active page.

Cream Postcard paper and the sticker sheet background.

Sand The dirt paths and warm neutrals.

Flag orange The frog’s travel flag, used for buttons and bushes.

Clover pink The pink clover flower, used for alternating sections.

Stamp yellow A postage-stamp accent for the banner.

Typography

Headings use Young Serif and body text uses Work Sans. Both have crisper edges than the earlier rounded typeface, so the page stays readable and a little more editorial while the illustrations carry the softness.

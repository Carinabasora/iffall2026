What is Travel Frog?

Travel Frog (旅かえる, Tabikaeru) is a free-to-play mobile game developed by Hit-Point Co., Ltd and released on 24 November 2017.

It is a low-pressure, non-competitive virtual pet game. You never control the frog. You only prepare for its journeys, then wait to see where it went.
